为什么不能做单篇论文概率滑块？
推断

给定调查的输出是重要性判断，没有提供论文 N 与实际复现成败的逐篇配对数据，也没有在这里给出经过验证的概率函数。仅移动 N 后显示一个概率会制造节选不支持的定量结论。

Survey study — recruitment, PDF page 10/15（材料所给）

To address this question, we surveyed psychological scientists, broadly defined, by sending out invitation links to a survey about “replicability in psychological research” through social media platforms and the Society for Personality and Social Psychology listserv.

Survey study — exclusion, PDF page 10/15（材料所给）

Ten undergraduate students replied to the survey and were excluded from all analyses, resulting in a final sample size of 215 psychological scientists.

Survey study — randomized presentation and outcome, PDF page 10/15（材料所给）

We randomized the order of presentation of nine study criteria between participants, and for each one, they rated the extent to which they thought it predicted a study’s replicability on a 5-point scale, with higher numbers signifying greater importance.

Survey study — outcome, PDF page 10/15（材料所给）

Respondents rated “large sample size” as the most important criterion (M = 3.84), judging it to be significantly more important than the second most important factor, “large effect size” (M = 3.61), t(214) = 3.21, p = 0.002.

Discussion — limitations, PDF page 12/15（材料所给）

This survey measures researchers’ judgments, not actual replication outcomes or the causal effect of sample size. Randomization concerns criterion presentation order, not assignment to sample-size interventions. The means, t statistic, and p value belong to respondents’ importance ratings. Do not infer causation, population representativeness, nine complete means, or replication rates from these excerpts.
